When I want a room to feel like a deep breath, this is the palette I reach for: soft sage florals, warm brass and lots of natural light.

**How the look works.** Start with one pattern you love, here a sage floral linen print, and echo it in a pleated floral lamp shade and a garden-style rug. Then add warmth with brass (a scalloped wall sconce and an hourglass that doubles as a little sculpture) and texture with natural rattan and wood. Creamy ceramics, like a ruffled bowl, keep it soft.

Every piece is from Walmart. Tap a number on the room to shop that piece, or scroll down for the full list.

*Styling tip:* a riser tray turns a few loose things on a coffee table, a book, a candle, a diffuser, into one tidy vignette.
