Every year I tell myself I'll write the Christmas cards early, and every year it's a late-night job at the kitchen table. So this year I'm giving it its own corner: good light, a warm drink and everything I need within reach.

**How the look works.** The cards set the palette. Two are from my own Zazzle store, Discover Art, made from my travel photos: the Munich Christmas market in front of the old town hall, and Big Ben glowing at night with a gold "Merry Christmas". Pick up those colours, soft blue sky, red rooftops and warm gold, in a personalized mug with the same Munich scene, and a golden doodle lamp that makes any desk feel cosy. Amber ribbed candleholders add that flicker of candlelight while you write.

The ornaments are the finishing touch, and they double as gifts: a Munich market photo ornament, a personalized family name ornament and, for the runners in your life, a "Run, Rest & Be Merry" run club ornament. Add your own names and year before you order.

The cards, mug and ornaments are from my Zazzle store, Discover Art, and the lamp and candleholders are from Walmart. Tap a number on the board to shop that piece, or scroll down for the full list.

*Styling tip:* keep a small tray or box for stamps, pens and your address list, so the card corner can stay set up all December and you can write a few each night with a cup of tea.
