There's something about a red tartan tablecloth that says Christmas straight away. Throw it over the table and the whole room feels ready for a long, cosy dinner.

**How the look works.** Let the tartan do the talking and keep everything else simple. Plain white stoneware plates and a white porcelain platter give the food room to shine, and woven natural napkins soften all that red. A trio of gold taper candle holders adds the glow, and flared stemless glasses keep the table relaxed rather than formal.

For dessert, a glass cake stand turns a shop-bought yule log into a centrepiece, and a glass drinks dispenser with a wooden lid means everyone can help themselves to punch or sparkling water. If it's your first Christmas somewhere new, the personalised "Our First Christmas in Our New Home" ornament from my Zazzle store, Discover Art, makes a lovely keepsake to hang nearby or tuck beside a place setting.

The tablecloth, candle holders, glasses, napkins and serveware are from Walmart, and the ornament is from my own Zazzle store. Tap a number on the board to shop that piece, or scroll down for the full list.

*Styling tip:* run a line of greenery and a few pine cones down the middle of the table, between the candles. It costs next to nothing and makes the tartan look even richer.
