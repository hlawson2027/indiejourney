This is the look I keep coming back to when the evenings get darker: warm, layered and soft, without feeling cluttered.

**The recipe is simple.** Start with one rich color, here a rust chunky knit throw. Add warm light from more than one source: a scalloped glass lamp, ribbed amber hurricanes and flameless candles you can leave glowing all evening. Then calm it all down with creamy ceramics, natural rattan and just a touch of gold.

Every piece is from Walmart, so it is easy to build the look slowly, one piece at a time. Tap a number on the room to shop that piece, or scroll down for the full list.
