If you know me, you know where my heart is headed: the Mediterranean. Until we get there, this is how I bring a little of it home.

Blue and white azulejo tiles are everywhere in Lisbon, on buildings, in cafés, on the street, and they never get old. Paired with bright lemons, they make a table feel like a sunny morning even in the middle of winter.

All of these are designs from my own Zazzle store, **Discover Art**. The serving tray and mugs make a great everyday set, the candle and star ornament make lovely gifts, and the gift tags finish off any present with a bit of Portugal.
