October should have been a terrible month for my Amazon storefront.

I ran the Chicago Marathon. Then I spent the days after it recovering, the way you do when your legs have just carried you 26.2 miles. And I was honestly wiped out from September, when I'd made over 200 videos in a single month for Data Coach Claire's challenge (which I won).

So in October, I spent **about two hours** on my Amazon storefront. The whole month. I posted **about 10 videos**, and that was it.

And it was my **best month ever: about $840**.

![September vs October 2026: over 200 videos and $589 in September, about 10 videos and $840 in October](/uploads/september-vs-october.jpg)

## Why it still worked

It wasn't luck. It was everything I'd set up before October, quietly working while I wasn't.

**My videos keep earning.** A video on an Amazon product page doesn't stop working when you stop posting. By October I had hundreds of videos on my storefront, including the 200+ from September, and they carried on earning commissions while I was running and resting. In October, my storefront commissions were about **$196**.

**Creator Connections campaigns kept paying.** These are campaigns where brands pay a higher commission for a set time. The campaigns I'd joined kept running in the background, and they brought in about **$638**, the biggest part of the month by far.

**My automations kept posting.** I've built systems so my content keeps going out without me sitting at a laptop: Pinterest pins scheduled through Make.com and Google Sheets, YouTube Shorts posted from my storefront videos, and an automated Facebook page. They don't take days off for marathons.

## What October taught me

**The work you do on a good day pays you on a bad day.** September was exhausting. But a lot of what I earned in October came from that effort. Hard months aren't wasted; they keep paying later.

**Build things that don't need you every day.** Life happens: races, recovery, family, illness, a busy season at work. If your income stops the moment you stop, you don't have a business, you have a job you can never take a break from.

**Rest is part of the plan.** I didn't feel guilty about October. I'd earned the break, and the systems I'd built meant I could take it.

## Why this matters for Spain

This is exactly the kind of income we need for our move. To meet the visa rules, Gavin and I need about **€3,300 a month (about $3,630)**, and it has to keep coming in while we live our lives: the afternoon naps, the hikes, the run adventures in Portugal. October was proof that it's possible. If you want the full numbers, I've shared them all in [How I've Earned $2,878 in Commissions So Far](/stories/my-affiliate-income-journey-so-far/).

You don't need to start with automations. Start with the thing that keeps working: content that keeps earning after you've made it. Then, little by little, put systems around it.
