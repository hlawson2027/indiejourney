If you didn't get round to Black Friday, don't panic. Cyber Monday is often just as good, especially for tech, gadgets and anything you'd normally buy online. And because we're saving hard for Spain, I treat it the same way I treat every sale: with a list, a budget and a cup of coffee, not a basket full of "bargains".

![A takeaway coffee cup on a wooden table in a cosy living room](/uploads/coffee-cosy-sofa-shopping.jpg)

## How to spot a real deal

- **Check the price history.** A "deal" that's the same price it was in October isn't a deal. Price-tracking tools and browser extensions show you a product's price over the last few months.
- **Compare Amazon and Walmart.** The same item is often on both, and the cheaper one changes by the hour.
- **Watch for "was" prices.** A huge percentage off a price nobody ever paid is a classic sale trick.
- **Read the recent reviews.** Sort by newest, so you see how the product is doing now, not three years ago.
- **Check the returns window.** Many retailers extend returns for holiday purchases, which takes the pressure off gifts.

## What I'd skip

- **Things you weren't going to buy anyway.** A deal on something you don't need is still money spent.
- **Bundles padded with filler.** Check what's actually in the box before you get excited about "free" extras.
- **The second version of something you already own.** One air fryer is enough.
- **Anything with a countdown clock that makes you panic.** If it's a good deal today, it'll probably be a good deal again at some point.
