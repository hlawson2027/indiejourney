Buying for a runner can feel tricky. They're picky about gear, they already own six pairs of shoes, and they'll tell you their GPS watch is "fine, actually." After the New York and Chicago Marathons and ultras in Peru and the French Alps, I've learned which gifts runners actually use, and which end up in the back of a drawer.

Here are my picks, from small stocking stuffers to the gifts they'll take to their next start line.
