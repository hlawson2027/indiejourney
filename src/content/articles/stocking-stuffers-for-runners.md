Runners are easy to buy for, as long as you skip the "funny" mugs. The best stocking stuffers are the small things runners use every week and are always running out of, losing or putting off buying for themselves.

After years of marathons and ultras in Peru and the French Alps, these are the little things I'd be happy to find in my stocking.

## Anti-chafe balm

Ask any runner: chafing is the enemy. A stick of **Body Glide** lives in every runner's kit bag, and you can never have too many (one for the bag, one for the car, one for race day).

[[product: body-glide-original-anti-chafe]]

## Really good running socks

Good socks are the one piece of kit runners never buy enough of. **Feetures** are cushioned and snug, and **Injinji toe socks** are the secret weapon for anyone who gets blisters between their toes.

[[product: feetures-elite-max-cushion-no-show]]

[[product: injinji-run-toesocks]]

## A running belt that doesn't bounce

Where do you put your phone, keys and gels? A slim **SPIbelt** sits flat under a top and doesn't bounce around.

[[product: spibelt-original-running-belt]]

## Race bib clips

Safety pins poke holes in your favourite top. **BibSNAPS** clip your race number on in seconds and can be used race after race. They're a great gift for anyone with a race booked for next year.

[[product: bibsnaps-race-bib-clips]]

## Massage balls

A set of **lacrosse balls** is the cheapest physio you'll ever buy. Roll them under your feet, sit on them for tight glutes, or lean into a wall for knotted shoulders.

[[product: lacrosse-massage-balls]]

## Reflective bands

For dark winter runs, slap-on **reflective bands** on the wrists and ankles make a runner much easier to see, because moving lights catch drivers' eyes.

[[product: goxrunx-reflective-bands]]

## A soft flask

A **soft flask** squashes down as you drink, so it doesn't slosh, and it fits in a pocket or vest. Perfect for long runs and trail races.

[[product: hydrapak-softflask]]

## For cold hands

Rechargeable **hand warmers** are a lovely gift for the runner who stands around at race starts, or the friend who comes to cheer.

[[product: rechargeable-hand-warmers]]
