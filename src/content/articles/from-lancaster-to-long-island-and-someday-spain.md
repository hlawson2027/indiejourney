Every magazine needs a first story, and this one has to be mine. So here's how I got here, and where I'm going.

## A big family and a sketchbook

I grew up in Lancaster, England, one of six kids raised by a single parent. It wasn't easy, but what I learned there has stayed with me my whole life: nobody is coming to hand you the life you want. You build it.

My way of building was drawing. I drew constantly, and that turned into a fashion design degree at the University of Central Lancashire. More than 25 years later I'm still a designer, and that habit of sketching an idea and then figuring out how to make it real runs through everything I do.

## Chasing a dream across an ocean

At 27, my husband and I did the brave, slightly reckless thing and moved to California to chase a dream we'd talked about for years. Our daughter was born there. She's 18 now, which I still can't quite believe.

Then a job brought us east, and Long Island became home. These days it's the three of us plus Jinx, our cat, who is very clear about who actually runs the house.

## Books under three names

I wrote my first novel, *War Kids*, after weeks of seeing the Syrian conflict on the news and not being able to stop thinking about the children caught in it. I didn't plan to become an author. I just needed to write that story.

Then I kept going. Dystopian worlds, alien invasions, time travel, a magical coven in Salem, and a series in Michael Anderle's Kurtherian Gambit universe. I've published as **Hayley Lawson**, **H.J. Lawson** and **Lawson**, and somewhere along the way I added coloring books and notebooks too. If you're curious, they're all on [my Books page](/books/).

## From first 5K to the French Alps

Running arrived later in life, and it rearranged everything. I ran the **New York City Marathon** and the **Chicago Marathon**, and then, because apparently that wasn't enough, I ran **ultramarathons in Peru and in the French Alps**.

I'm in my forties and I'm not an elite athlete. I'm a regular woman who fits training around work, family and everything else. If that's you too, you're in the right place. I'll be sharing the gear I actually use, the training that actually works for real life, and the races that changed me. Find it all in [Running Adventures](/running-adventures/).

## A landlord in two countries

I'm also a landlord, with rentals in the **UK and Chicago**. Managing homes across an ocean comes with its own set of challenges, and I'll be writing the honest version in [Landlord Diaries](/landlord-diaries/).

## The side businesses

Over the years I've built a handful of small businesses alongside my work: my [Amazon storefront](/shop/amazon/) with hundreds of product videos, my [Walmart finds](/shop/walmart/), **Discover Art**, my Zazzle store of travel photography and designs, and **Pintanstic**, a Pinterest automation tool I built for Amazon Influencers. They're all on [My Stores](/stores/).

## Where it's all heading

Here's the big goal behind all of it: my husband and I are working toward **retiring to Spain**. Every book, rental and side project is a step on that road. I'll be documenting the plan, the research and, one day, the move itself in [The Spain Diaries](/spain-diaries/).

## Why I started The Indie Journey

My life has never fit in one box. I'm a designer, a writer, a runner, a landlord, a mum and a small business owner, For years those pieces lived in separate places. The Indie Journey is where they finally come together.

This is a magazine for anyone building a life their own way, whether that's training for a first race, publishing a first book, buying a first rental or quietly planning a big move. I'm not an expert who has it all figured out. I'm just a few steps further down the road, and I'm happy to share the map.

Thanks for being here. Let's go.

*Hayley*
