*Photos: me and Tugce.*

If you've read my [MDS Peru Diaries](/stories/mds-peru-part-1-looking-for-my-limit/), you'll know Tugce. She was my roommate in Lima, my running partner across the Ica Desert, and the friend I promised I'd see again. She lives in France, so [when and why I went], Paris was where we met up.

## Arriving

I landed at Charles de Gaulle and found Tugce on the train into the city, still smiling, still the same.

![Tugce and me on the train at Charles de Gaulle](/uploads/paris-img_5060.jpg)

## Rain, and then a rainbow

Paris gave us a proper downpour. We didn't have an umbrella between us, so Tugce did what any desert-trained woman would do and improvised: a striped scarf over her head and onward.

![Caught in the rain, scarf as an umbrella](/uploads/paris-img_5063.jpg)

The best thing about Paris rain is what comes after. The streets turned to mirrors, the stone glowed gold, and then a rainbow appeared over the rooftops. Tugce could not stop laughing.

![A rainbow over a Paris street corner, Tugce laughing in the foreground](/uploads/paris-img_5072.jpg)

![Wet streets and the church at the end of the road](/uploads/paris-img_5076.jpg)

I loved walking with no plan and finding views at the end of the streets, like this one, with a church framed by Haussmann buildings and Sacré-Cœur sitting on the hill behind it.

![Looking up the street to Sacré-Cœur on the hill](/uploads/paris-img_5079.jpg)

## La Madeleine

La Madeleine looks like a Greek temple dropped into the middle of Paris: a wall of huge columns on the outside, and inside, domes, gold, chandeliers and marble. We were stopped in our tracks.

![La Madeleine from the street](/uploads/paris-img_5105.jpg)

![Inside La Madeleine, looking down the nave](/uploads/paris-img_5106.jpg)

![Tugce looking up at the baptism sculpture](/uploads/paris-img_5108.jpg)

![The altar and the painted dome](/uploads/paris-img_5109.jpg)

## Friends from the desert

When I left Peru, I wondered whether I'd ever see these people again. Walking around Paris with Tugce, soaked one minute and under a rainbow the next, felt like proof that the friendships you make when you're pushed to your limit are the ones that last.
