## The big pieces

### 1. A kitchen island with storage and seating

It adds extra counter space, two drawers, open shelves and hooks for the things that used to clutter the worktop, plus room to pull up two stools. If your kitchen has a gap in the middle or along one wall, an island like this makes it feel like a proper hub.

[[product: kitchen-island-with-storage-and-seating]]

### 2. Blue velvet swivel bar stools

Cosy, comfortable and a gorgeous colour. The velvet and gold legs make the island feel a bit more "restaurant" and a bit less "storage unit", and the swivel means nobody has to shuffle the stool back to get up. The height adjusts too, so they work at the island or a counter.

[[product: velvet-swivel-bar-stools-set-of-4]]

**Designer tip:** a statement colour on seating is the easiest way to add personality to a neutral kitchen. It's much cheaper to swap stools than cabinets.

## The matcha corner

I've been making a lot of matcha lately and comparing a few ceremonial-grade powders.

### 9. HANA MATCHA First Flush ceremonial matcha

A Japanese ceremonial matcha with a bright green colour. I've tasted it side by side with other powders, and it's a lovely one to whisk up plain.

[[product: hana-matcha-first-flush-ceremonial]]

### 10. OSULLOC Volcanic Isle ceremonial matcha

A Korean matcha from Jeju island. This is the one I've been using for my matcha lattes.

[[product: osulloc-volcanic-isle-matcha]]

**My simple matcha latte:** sift a teaspoon of matcha into a mug, whisk it with a splash of hot (not boiling) water until smooth, then top up with warm frothed milk. Add honey if you like it sweet.
