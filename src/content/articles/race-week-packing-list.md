I've now run two of the World Marathon Majors, New York and Chicago, and I've learned the hard way that race week isn't the time to try anything new. Chicago was a 48-hour dash with a delayed flight and a race-day forecast that was hotter than anyone expected, so packing light and packing right really mattered.

This is my list. Everything with a link is something I've featured on my Amazon storefront, from my go-to running top to the vest I carry my water in.

## The little things that save race morning

These are the things people forget, and they matter more than any gadget:

- **Safety pins** for your bib (bring extras)
- **Your bib, ID and any race confirmation** in one place
- **Passport and boarding pass** if you're flying out after, like I was
- **Snacks** for the morning, plus something you know you can eat before the start
- **Phone and charger** (and a way to carry the phone while you run)
- **Throwaway layers** for waiting in the corral; many races collect and donate them
- **A plan B for getting to the start.** In Chicago I switched from the train to an Uber at the last minute and walked the last 15 minutes in the dark. I arrived with plenty of time, which is exactly how you want to feel.
