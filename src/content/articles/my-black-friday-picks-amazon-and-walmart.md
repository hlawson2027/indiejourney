Black Friday can be brilliant or it can be a black hole for your bank balance. We're saving hard for our move to Spain, so I go in with a list, not a basket. These are the things I think are genuinely worth waiting for a sale on: the things you'd buy anyway, that get used every day, and that cost enough for a discount to matter.

## My rules for shopping the sales

- **Make the list before the sales start.** If it wasn't on your list last week, you probably don't need it.
- **Buy the "big once" things.** Black Friday is the time for the watch, the vacuum or the coffee machine, not for a basket of small bits.
- **Check what you already own.** Two air fryers are not better than one.
- **Think about gifts now.** If someone on your list has been hinting, this is when to grab it.
