For four days in the Ica Desert, everything I needed lived in one 20-litre bag. Here's exactly what went in it, what worked, and what I'd change. You can shop it all in one place in my Amazon idea list:

## The pack and sleep system

1. **WAA Ultra Bag 20L:** the official MDS pack, with bottles on the front straps. It isn't sold on Amazon, so my list has the Ultimate Direction Fastpack 20, a similar 20-litre vest-style pack.
2. **Sleeping bag:** desert nights get cold, so don't skimp here. Mine was a Millet bag; my list has a lightweight Naturehike down bag that packs small.
3. **Foam sleeping mat:** light, folds flat and doubles as a seat in camp (Therm-a-Rest Z Lite Sol).
4. **Trekking poles:** lifesavers on the sand mountain on stage two.

![My packed race bag, poles and sleeping bag](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8096.jpg)

## Food and fuel

5. **Freeze-dried meals:** single-serve meals from Peak Refuel and Backpacker's Pantry. You need at least 2,000 calories a day.
6. **Maurten gels:** easy fuel on the move.
7. **Electrolyte sachets:** for every bottle.
8. **Esbit stove and fuel tablets:** pack something to block the wind, because my flame kept blowing out.

![Four days of race food laid out](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8085.jpg)

## On my body

9. **Knee support:** I wore it for the entire race.
10. **Sand gaiters:** mine let me down, so choose a well-fitting pair and test them on real sand.
11. **Salomon trail shoes:** my upgrade for next time, after the wrong shoes wrecked my feet. Go a size up for swelling.

![My race clothes laid out on the bed](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8087.jpg)

## Care kit

12. **Blister kit:** learn to use it before you go.
13. **Anti-chafe balm and sunscreen.**

![My care kit: sunscreen, balm, plasters and more](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8089.jpg)

## What I'd change

Go up a shoe size and train in your exact race shoes and gaiters on real sand. Pack a windbreak for your stove. And bring an orange: trust me.
