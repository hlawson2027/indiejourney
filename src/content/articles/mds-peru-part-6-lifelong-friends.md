*MDS Peru Diaries, Part 6. Photos: me and Tugce.*

In [Part 5](/stories/mds-peru-part-5-the-desert-made-me/) I crossed the finish line with my new friends and left an old fear behind in the desert. But the adventure wasn't quite over yet.

## Stranded in the desert (again)

After the finish we all piled onto a coach to drive through the desert to our hotel, The Legend Paracas Resort. Halfway there, the coach got stuck in the sand. After everything we'd just done, we were stranded in the desert one more time. Some of us climbed out to push, while everyone else sat in the sand and waited, until a line of MDS 4x4s turned up and towed us out.

![One last look at the finish line from the coach](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8348.jpg)

![Everyone pushing the coach stuck in the sand](/uploads/mds-peru-img_3974.jpg)

![The 4x4s coming to the rescue](/uploads/mds-peru-img_3981.jpg)

## One room, one bed

When we arrived, the hotel had put Tugce and me in different rooms. We'd spent the whole adventure together and wanted to share the last part too, so we asked the hotel to put us in the same room. They did, and then we found out why it had been so easy: they thought we were a couple, so they'd given us one bed.

Tugce, being the superstar she is, sorted the room out for us while I went to see the foot doctor, one of the wonderful orange-jacket medical team.

Once we'd showered, it was finally time to celebrate properly: medal on, Legend slippers on, and a finisher's bracelet engraved with MDS 120 PERU on my wrist.

![Me with my medal in the hotel room](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8380.jpg)

![My MDS 120 Peru finisher's bracelet](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8383.jpg)

## The foot doctor and the buffet

It was really hard to walk by then. I wasn't the only one: lots of people had the same problem, and some were worse off than me, with infections.

![The MDS foot team patching me up at the hotel](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8367.jpg)

That night MDS put on a buffet, and after days of freeze-dried food it was wonderful. I also noticed I'd swollen up a bit from the whole adventure, which apparently is very normal after a race like this.

We made a plan to head into town the next day, and then we slept like babies.

![Tugce and me with drinks at the hotel](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8369.jpg)

![The hotel pool under a full moon](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8371.jpg)

## Bookmarks and a boat ride

The next day we wandered around Paracas, browsing the market stalls. Tugce bought us all bookmarks to remember the trip. We ate great food, laughed and chilled.

![The girls squeezed into the back of a taxi](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8384.jpg)

![Tugce at the Paracas market stalls](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8393.jpg)

![Yuk on the Paracas seafront](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8394.jpg)

![Ceviche and lunch together](/uploads/mds-peru-img_4023.jpg)

Instead of a taxi back, we decided to take a boat to the hotel, which was so much fun. Not many people turn up at their hotel by boat.

![The girls on the Puerto Ballestas pier](/uploads/mds-peru-img_4044.jpg)

![Windswept selfie on the boat](/uploads/mds-peru-img_4050.jpg)

## The awards night

That evening was the big event. MDS puts on an awards show, followed by a film of the race, and my photo was in it!

My feet were still taped up, so I swapped trainers for sandals, and I definitely wasn't the only one.

![The MDS 120 Peru awards stage](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8420.jpg)

![Bandaged toes in sandals at the awards](/uploads/mds-peru-img_4085.jpg)

Then it turned into a party, with loads of great food, wine included, and music that felt like Eurovision. I gave one of the waiters a good tip, and for the rest of the night he was our personal waiter. We danced all night. It was so much fun.

![The dance floor in full swing](/uploads/mds-peru-img_8423.jpg)

![Tugce, me and a new friend at the party](/uploads/mds-peru-img_4092.jpg)

## The long road home

The next morning was less fun: a long coach ride back to Lima with a lot of very tired people.

When we got to the airport, I felt sad. Would I stay in touch with these amazing people? Would Tugce message me back?

## What the desert taught me

This adventure taught me that I can do anything I put my mind to.

It also showed me what I'd been missing. My family moved to America for work and money, and we didn't really think about the sacrifices we were making. Time. Time we missed with family, with friends, and time not spent making new friends.

My feelings about that are mixed. Those 20 years of hard work have built a great foundation for me, my husband Gavin and our daughter Callie, and they'll let us retire early and get our time back.

But I came home knowing I want more people like this in my life.

And yes, Tugce did message me back. Now we message nearly every day, often lots of times a day. There are plenty more Tugce and Zuzanna stories to come. They're lifelong friends now.

*The End … for now.*
