Everyone has that person. Their camera roll is mostly the dog, the cat has its own birthday, and they will happily tell you about both. We have a cat called Jinx, so I know exactly who I'm shopping for here.

Pet lovers are surprisingly easy to buy for: anything with *their* animal on it wins. So this guide starts with portraits and ends with a few small, fun extras.

## Custom pet portraits

These are from Kindred Tails Studio, my new Etsy shop (so yes, I'm biased). You send a photo of the pet and get a finished digital portrait back, ready to print, frame or use as a phone wallpaper. There's nothing to ship, which makes them a brilliant last-minute gift. Check each listing for the current turnaround time.

The royal portrait is the showstopper: your dog or cat painted as a king or queen, crown and all.

[[product: kindred-royal-pet-portrait]]

For someone with a more minimal home, the line art version is a simple one-line drawing with a little heart worked in.

[[product: kindred-line-art-pet-portrait]]

If their pet has a big personality, go for the cartoon. It's bright and bold, and it makes me smile every time.

[[product: kindred-cartoon-pet-portrait]]

And because pets are family, there are two watercolour options with the people in too: one for the whole family, and one for a couple and their pet. The couple portrait doubles as a Valentine's gift if you're organised enough to think that far ahead.

[[product: kindred-couple-pet-portrait]]
