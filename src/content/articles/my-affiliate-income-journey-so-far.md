When I wrote about [why we're moving to Spain](/stories/why-we-are-moving-to-spain/), I promised to be open about the numbers. To get our visa, Gavin and I need about **€3,300 a month (about $3,630)** coming in, and a big part of that plan is my affiliate income. So here it is: what I've actually earned, where it came from, and what hasn't worked yet.

[[spain-tracker]]

## Where I started: years of pennies

I've had an Amazon Associates account since 2020. For years it earned me pennies: 45 cents one month, $1.60 another, 27 cents one Christmas.

That changed this year. My first real month from my Amazon storefront was **June 2026, when I earned $109**. Since then, it's grown every month except November, which isn't finished yet.

![Chart of monthly commissions from June to November 2026: $109, $285, $520, $589, $840 and $535 so far in November](/uploads/affiliate-income-by-month.jpg)

## What the different kinds of income mean

If you're thinking of doing this yourself, it helps to know there's more than one way to earn:

- **Storefront commissions** come from my Amazon Influencer storefront and the videos I make for product pages on Amazon. When someone watches a video and buys, I earn a commission.
- **Creator Connections** are campaigns where brands pay a higher commission for a set time. These have been the biggest part of my income by far.
- **Offsite links** are the links I share outside Amazon: on Pinterest, here on the blog and on social media.
- **Walmart** is my Walmart Creator storefront and collections.

## August: getting help

In August, I signed up for **Data Coach Claire's course**. I'm honest about this: I'm working through it slowly, around the rentals, running and everything else. But it made me look at my storefront as a business, using data rather than guessing.

## September: 200 videos in a month

In September, Claire ran a challenge, and I went all in. I made **over 200 videos in one month**, while also working hard on my pins and idea lists. **And I won the challenge.**

It was a lot of work, but you can see what it did in the chart: September was my best month at the time, and **October was my best month ever at about $840** (about €764).

## Where the money actually came from

![Breakdown of commissions from June to November 2026: Creator Connections $2,079.86 (72.3%), storefront commissions $765.08 (26.6%), offsite Amazon links $31.85 (1.1%) and Walmart $1.22](/uploads/affiliate-income-split.jpg)

Here's the honest picture. Almost **three-quarters of my income comes from Creator Connections**, and nearly all of the rest comes from my storefront on Amazon. My offsite links have earned **$31.85** this year. That's tiny, but November is already my best offsite month ever at over $23, and it's not over yet.

## Being honest about Walmart

I'd love to tell you Walmart is going well, but it isn't yet. I joined Walmart Creator in April 2024, and so far my links have driven **$27.18 in sales, which earned me $1.22**. That's not a typo.

I've now built 11 collections and I'm adding new ones every day, but Walmart is a slow burn for me. The traffic just hasn't turned into orders yet. I'm sharing it because I think too many people only post their wins, and this is part of the journey too.

## What's next: the offsite push

Right now nearly all of my income comes from Amazon itself, and brand campaigns come and go. So the next big goal is to build my **offsite income up to $1,000 a month**. Here's the plan:

- **Pinterest every day.** New pins for my lists, stories and lookbooks.
- **New lists every day.** A themed Amazon idea list, a Walmart collection and a Zazzle collection, each with 10 products.
- **This website.** Real stories (like this one) that link to the things I recommend.
- **Keep making videos.** I'm past 700 on my storefront, and they keep earning long after I make them.
- **Keep learning.** I'll keep working through Claire's course, a module at a time.
