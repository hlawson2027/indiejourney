Hi, I'm Hayley, and welcome to The Indie Journey.

## Where it started

I was born in Lancaster, England, one of six kids raised by a single parent. It wasn't easy, but it taught me early that if you want something, you build it yourself. I was always the one drawing, and that love of making things took me to the University of Central Lancashire, where I earned a BA (Hons) in fashion design. I've worked as a designer ever since, for more than 25 years now.

At 27, my husband Gavin and I packed up and moved to California to chase a long-held dream. Our daughter, Callie, was born there. She's 18 now and heading to university in England. A job later brought us to New York, and Long Island has been home ever since, along with our cat, Jinx, who runs the house.

## The writer

My first novel, *War Kids*, started with the images of the Syrian conflict I kept seeing on the news. I couldn't stop thinking about what war does to children, so I wrote about it. One book became a series, and one series became many.

Today I've written dystopian, sci-fi and magical adventures under three names: **Hayley Lawson**, **H.J. Lawson** and **Lawson**. That includes *War Kids*, *The Sanction Series*, *Invasion Survivor*, *Live Free or Die* in Michael Anderle's Kurtherian Gambit universe, and *13 Covens*, plus a shelf of adult coloring books and notebooks. You'll find every one of them on [my Books page](/books/).

## The runner

Running came later, and it changed everything. I've crossed the finish line at the **New York City and Chicago Marathons**, and then I went further: I've run **ultramarathons in Peru and in the French Alps**. I still love a half marathon, a Hyrox and a good training block for whatever's next. I'm proof you don't have to start young to start strong.

I test a lot of running gear, fitness finds and everyday products along the way, and I share the ones that actually earn their place, with honest reviews and real-life testing.

## The landlord

I'm also a landlord, with rental properties in the **UK and Chicago**. Managing homes across an ocean has taught me a lot, and I'll be sharing the real side of it, the good and the not-so-glamorous, in Landlord Diaries.

## The maker and the business side

Somewhere between the books and the miles, I started building small businesses of my own:

- **My Amazon storefront**, with hundreds of product videos and idea lists for runners, home, travel and gifts
- **My Walmart finds**, for the good stuff at a good price
- **Discover Art**, my Zazzle store of travel photography and designs on gifts and home pieces
- **Pintanstic**, a Pinterest pin automation service I built for Amazon Influencers

You can find them all on [My Stores](/stores/).

## The Spain plan

All of this is building toward one big goal: Gavin and I are working toward retiring to the **Alicante area of Spain** in the next few years. Every book, rental and side business is a step on that road. I've shared the why, the real numbers and our progress tracker in [Why We're Moving to Spain](/stories/why-we-are-moving-to-spain/), and I'm writing about the whole journey in [The Spain Diaries](/spain-diaries/).

## Why The Indie Journey

I've never followed a straight line. This magazine is the home for all of it: design, books, running, home and living, travel, rentals, the businesses, the road to Spain, and the honest middle bits in between. It's for anyone building a life their own way, one step (or one mile) at a time.
