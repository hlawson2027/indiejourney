# The Indie Journey — Owner Guide

Your dashboard: **https://app.pagescms.org** → sign in with GitHub → open **indiejourney**.
It works the same on your phone and your laptop. Every **Save** publishes to the website in about two minutes.

## The 10 things you'll do most

1. **Write a story** — Stories → **Add an entry**. Fill in Title, Category, Publish date, Cover photo, Summary and Story. Leave **Draft** ticked while you work, then untick it and Save to publish.
2. **Save a draft** — keep **Draft** ticked and Save. It's stored but hidden from the public site.
3. **Preview before publishing** — drafts appear on your private preview site (link in Setup notes below) about two minutes after you Save.
4. **Schedule a story** — untick Draft and pick a future Publish date. The site rebuilds every morning at about 5:15 a.m. New York time and the story appears that day.
5. **Add photos** — tap any photo field and upload from your camera roll. The site resizes and compresses them for you. Always fill in the short photo description.
6. **Add a product** — Products → **Add an entry**: name, store, affiliate link, photo, short description. Tick "Show in the homepage shop band" to feature it.
7. **Put products in a story** — either pick them under "Shop this post" (they appear as cards at the end), or type `[[product: Product name]]` on its own line in the story to place a card exactly there.
8. **Add a YouTube video** — paste the YouTube link on its own line in a story, or into "Featured YouTube video".
9. **Build a lookbook** — Lookbooks → **Add an entry**: room photo, then add numbered dots. Each dot picks a product and a position: Across 0–100 (left to right) and Down 0–100 (top to bottom). Save, check the preview, nudge the numbers if a dot is off.
10. **Change the homepage** — Homepage: choose the lead story, the two side stories, and drag the sections into any order. Add a new section with **Add**.

## Everything else

- **New category**: Categories → Add. It gets its own page at `/category-name/`. Add it to the menu in **Site settings → Main menu**.
- **Menu, social links, footer**: Site settings.
- **Books and stores**: Books, and Stores & Businesses. New ones appear on /books/ and /stores/ automatically.
- **About, Disclosures, Privacy**: Pages.
- **Publish immediately** (for example a scheduled post you want out now): the **Publish site now** button on the Stories page.
- **Stats**: Cloudflare dashboard (or the Cloudflare phone app) → Analytics & Logs → Web Analytics.
- **Undo a mistake**: every save is kept forever. Ask Claude to "restore the previous version of <page>".

## Rules of thumb

- Upload phone-size photos, not huge camera originals.
- Product photos must be your own, or ones the retailer's program allows you to use.
- Don't write exact prices in product notes; they change. "Under $30" is fine.
- The affiliate disclosure is added automatically to any post with product cards, and the Amazon statement is in the footer.

## If something looks wrong

- The site didn't update: wait five minutes, then press **Publish site now**.
- A save failed with a red error: usually a required field is empty (Title, Category, Publish date, or a product link that doesn't start with https://).
- A page shows "uses a web address that is already taken": a category and a page have the same name. Rename one.
